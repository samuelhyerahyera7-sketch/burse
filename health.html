Getting started

Three steps to your first session

You can be matched with a therapist and have your first session within 48 hours of signing up.

1
Tell us about yourself
Answer a short questionnaire about your situation, preferences, and goals. It takes about five minutes and is entirely confidential.
2
Get matched with a therapist
Our matching process considers your needs, therapist specialties, availability, and communication preferences. You can switch at any time, for any reason.
3
Start therapy your way
Connect by messaging, voice note, phone call, or live video — whichever fits your comfort and schedule. Therapy should work around you, not the other way around.

Yes — every therapist on the platform holds an active licence from their state or national regulatory body. All are Masters- or Doctorate-level clinicians with at least three years of post-qualification experience. We verify credentials before any therapist can see clients.
All sessions — whether text, voice, or video — are end-to-end encrypted. We are HIPAA-compliant and will never sell your data. Your therapist cannot share anything you discuss without your explicit consent, except in the narrow circumstances required by law (such as imminent risk of harm).
Most members are matched with a therapist within 24 hours of completing the questionnaire. You can have your first session — whether a message exchange or a live call — within 48 hours of signing up.
Yes — at any time, for any reason, at no extra cost. A good therapeutic relationship matters, and we want you to find the right fit. You can request a new match directly from your account and will typically be rematched within 24 hours.
We offer reduced rates for people who can't afford full-price therapy. When signing up, you'll be asked about your financial situation — if you qualify, you'll be offered a discounted rate automatically. We believe cost should never be the reason someone doesn't get support.
