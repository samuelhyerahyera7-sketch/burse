Getting started

Therapy in three simple steps

You can be matched with a therapist and have your first session within 48 hours of signing up.

1
Answer a few questions
Tell us about what you're going through, your preferences, and your goals. It takes about five minutes and is completely confidential.
2
Get matched with a therapist
We match you based on your needs and therapist specialties. You're free to switch therapists at any time, for any reason, at no extra cost.
3
Start therapy your way
Connect by message, voice note, phone call, or live video — on your schedule. Therapy built around your life, not the other way around.
