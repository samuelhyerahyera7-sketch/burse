Monthly reconciliation (EMP201)
EMP201 is calculated from an approved pay run — draft runs aren't included since their figures can still change. Approve a pay run first, then come back here.
Annual reconciliation (EMP501)

Reconciles the tax year's monthly EMP201 liabilities against the annual total and the number of IRP5/IT3(a) certificates issuable — for capturing on SARS e@syFile during the reconciliation window.

Employee cost report

Full cost to company per employee for a pay run — gross pay plus employer UIF and SDL, less any Employment Tax Incentive claimed.

Payroll audit trail

Every payroll approval, bank file download, payslip email and accountant access change for this company.

Tax certificates (IRP5)

A per-employee summary of the year's figures in SARS IRP5 source-code format, for completing or checking the official submission in e@syFile. This is a reference certificate — Burse does not submit to SARS.

Payroll & tax settings
Tax year and SARS tax tables (PAYE brackets, UIF ceiling, ETI bands) are applied automatically based on the pay date — there's nothing to configure here.

Leave policy

Offer more than the BCEA minimum if you like — Burse will never let you go below it. Leave blank to use the BCEA minimum for your work week.

Company bank reference — informational only. Burse never moves money; payments happen via the CSV you export from Bank Payment Files, uploaded to your own business banking.

Owner
Full access — manages employees, runs and approves payroll, downloads bank files, edits banking details, invites accountants.
Accountant
Read-only — can view payslips, reports and bank payment files. Cannot approve payroll, edit bank details, or change salaries.

Run Payroll

Bonus / Once-off Payment

A once-off amount for this pay run only — it never changes the employee's ongoing salary, and is taxed correctly as an irregular payment (not multiplied by 12).

New Loan / Salary Advance

Deducted automatically from this employee's payroll each period until the balance reaches zero. One active loan per employee at a time.
